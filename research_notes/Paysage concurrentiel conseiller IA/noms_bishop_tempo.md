## 5. Connotations and SEO difficulty

### Takeaway
"Bishop": strong religious first meaning in English (and live 2026 news linking "bishops" and "AI"), sci-fi association with Alien's trustworthy android Bishop (arguably positive for an AI), and in French the piece is "le fou" (also "the madman") — the chess link does not survive translation. "Tempo": positive, energetic, very generic (music, sport, fitness, payments, Jira), chess meaning only known to players; SEO near-impossible as a single word.

### Cited Findings
- Bishop in Alien franchise: android played by Lance Henriksen, first in *Aliens* (1986), the "good" synthetic contrasting with Ash — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishop_(Aliens)); [Den of Geek](https://www.denofgeek.com/movies/alien-androids-ai-now-center-of-the-franchise/); Alien franchise in 2025–26 centers on androids/AI — [Den of Geek](https://www.denofgeek.com/movies/alien-androids-ai-now-center-of-the-franchise/)
- Religious SEO collision: November 2026 coverage of the U.S. Catholic bishops' new AI task force ("US Catholic bishops' new AI task force urges regulation…") dominates "bishop AI" queries — [Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/nov/18/us-catholic-bishops-new-ai-task-force-urges-regulation-worker/); [Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/11/18/nation/catholic-bishops-ai-task-force/); [Catholic World Report](https://www.catholicworldreport.com/2026/09/16/u-s-bishops-launch-task-force-to-promote-human-dignity-amid-rise-of-artificial-intelligence/)
- French: the bishop is called "fou" only in French (Läufer in German, alfil in Spanish, from Arabic al-fil "elephant") — [Coddy](https://coddy.tech/chess/fr/concepts/bishop); [Chess.com FR "Fou"](https://www.chess.com/fr/terms/fou-echecs); academic study "Le fou est-il vraiment fou ?" (Dębowiak, 2013) — [CEEOL](https://ceeol.com/search/article-detail?id=19938)
- "Tempo" namespace in search is shared by Stripe's blockchain, Jira's top time-tracking app, Grafana's tracing tool, a $300M fitness brand, a calendar app and an AI app builder — see section 1 sources.

### Inferences
- Bishop + chess personality: coherent for English speakers (diagonal, long-range, "sees angles others don't" — fits "visionary"), but the piece is a minor piece, not the strategist; the religious meaning will be the default reading for non-chess users. For French consultants, "Fou" = madman could be either a playful "fou génial" angle or a credibility risk; the English name "Bishop" in France reads as "évêque" first.
- Tempo + chess: elegant concept ("gain a tempo" = stay a move ahead), but almost no non-chess player will connect it; in English it reads as "pace/rhythm" — which collides precisely with Tempo Software's time-tracking/PMO meaning.
- SEO: "Bishop" is hard (religion, Bishop Fox, surname, place names) but modifiable ("Bishop AI advisor"); "Tempo" is nearly impossible to rank for alone and "Tempo AI" is already a Salesforce-owned domain, a Chrome extension and the Stripe blockchain's AI narrative.

### Gaps
- No keyword volume / SERP difficulty data (no SEO tool access). Search-intent conclusions are qualitative.

## 6. Suggested variants (5–10 per direction) and obvious conflicts

### Takeaway
Pure "Tempo" should be abandoned or heavily compounded; "Bishop" works better as a compound. Chess-term alternatives like Fianchetto and Zwischenzug look largely free in software but are hard to spell; Gambit, Checkmate, Endgame and Zugzwang are already used.

### Cited Findings
- No AI startup/software named "Fianchetto" or "Zwischenzug" found; only a 2019 open-source chess server called fianchetto on GitHub — [awesome.ecosyste.ms](https://awesome.ecosyste.ms/projects/github.com%2Fguillotinaweb%2Ffianchetto)
- "Zugzwang" is used by a chess-improvement tool (waitlist, June 2026) — [PeerPush](https://peerpush.com/p/zugzwang-zzmp)
- "Checkmate" used by an a16z Speedrun stealth AI vertical SaaS startup and by a "Checkmate AI" iOS app; "Endgame AI" is an iOS chess app — [a16z Speedrun](https://speedrun.a16z.com/companies/checkmate); [App Store Endgame AI](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6755304651)
- "Gambit" is crowded in AI/consulting: Gambit (gambitco.io, AI "workers", NVIDIA Inception), Gambit Group AI (San Diego AI strategy consulting), GAMBIT defense consulting arm of AlphaBriefing — [gambitco.io](https://gambitco.io/about-us); [SD Chamber](https://chamber.sdbusinesschamber.com/list/member/gambit-group-ai-38435); [gambit-web](https://gambit-web.pages.dev/)

### Inferences (not cleared — each needs a search)
**Bishop direction**
1. **Bishop's Move** / **BishopMove** — descriptive, distinct from Bishop Fox; no conflict found (not searched specifically).
2. **Long Diagonal** / **Diagonal** — evokes the bishop's reach ("sees across the board"); "Diagonal" is a generic word likely used elsewhere.
3. **Fianchetto** — the bishop's signature development; no software conflict found; spelling/pronunciation hard for non-chess audiences.
4. **Bishop Pair** / **The Bishop Pair** — chess concept of a lasting strategic advantage; no conflict found (not searched).
5. **Bishop Advisor** / **Bishop for Consultants** — clearer, but small "Bishop AI" consultancies and "Bishop for Advisors" (bishopforadvisors.com) are close — moderate risk.
6. **Alfil** (Spanish/Arabic for bishop, "the elephant") — neutral in English and French, avoids religion and "fou"; not searched.
7. **Lance** (Henriksen/Alien nod is subtle; also a chess-free "lance") — likely crowded generic; not recommended without search.

**Tempo direction**
1. **Zwischenzug** ("in-between move") — distinctive, no conflict found; very hard to spell/pronounce — better as a feature name.
2. **Extra Tempo** / **Up a Tempo** — idiomatic chess, but still contains TEMPO → conflicts with Tempo ehf in class 9/42; high risk.
3. **Tempo Advisor** — contains dominant element TEMPO; high risk vs Tempo Software (same consultants/PMO customers). Not recommended.
4. **Initiative** — chess concept close to tempo (having the initiative); generic English word, hard to protect.
5. **Prophylaxis** / **Prophy** — chess strategic concept (anticipating the opponent's plan) fitting a "visionary" advisor; not searched.
6. **Outpost** — strategic square; likely used in software (not searched).
7. **Quiet Move** / **Intermezzo** (Italian synonym of zwischenzug) — Intermezzo is a known music/term, may be used; not searched.

### Gaps
- None of the variants were trademark-searched individually; only Fianchetto/Zwischenzug/Zugzwang/Checkmate/Endgame/Gambit got a web check. Each shortlisted variant needs USPTO/EUIPO/INPI knockout searches and domain checks.
