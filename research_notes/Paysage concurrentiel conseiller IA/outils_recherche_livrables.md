## 1. Research / analysis platforms: what they do, who they target, funding, pricing, traction, advice vs research

### Takeaway
The research layer is well funded and consolidating: AlphaSense (about $600M ARR, $7.5B valuation, June 2026) is the category giant, followed by finance-specialised agents Rogo (about $2B valuation, April 2026) and Hebbia (last priced at $700M in 2024). All of them now sell "agents" that go from search to drafted memos and decks, but their positioning is still evidence retrieval and synthesis with citations, not opinionated strategic recommendations. Seat prices are five-figure per year for the premium players versus $10-40/month for general research assistants.

### Inferences
- The research layer is turning into "content + agent" businesses whose moat is proprietary data (AlphaSense/Tegus transcripts, broker research; Third Bridge transcripts). - Finance-specialised agents (Rogo, Hebbia) already go from question to memo and deck; their next logical step is "investment view / recommendation", but their buyer is banks and funds, not strategy consultants.
- Budget overlap: a corporate strategy team paying $15-20k/seat for AlphaSense plus $30k for Klue/Crayon has budget; independents mostly live on $20-40/month general assistants.

## 2. Slide / deliverable tools: what they do, funding, pricing, traction

### Takeaway
Gamma is the breakout ($100M+ ARR, $2.1B valuation, profitable, Nov 2025); Tome exited the category to become an AI CRM (Lightfield); Prezent ($400M valuation, Oct 2025) is the enterprise-communication player; think-cell and UpSlide own the consulting/finance PowerPoint add-in niche and are adding AI. Microsoft (Copilot) and Anthropic (Claude for PowerPoint, GA May 2026) now ship native PowerPoint agents, squeezing standalone tools. None of these tools positions itself as giving strategic advice; they format and draft.

## 3. Finance-light analysis tools used by consultants (Excel copilots, planning)

### Takeaway
The Excel layer is contested by Microsoft (Copilot Agent/Edit Mode, $30/user/month add-on), Anthropic (Claude in Excel, GA May 2026) and startups like Shortcut (Fundamental Research Labs, about $40M raised). Planning platforms like Pigment ($2.3B valuation, Oct 2026) are moving to agents that "run the plan". These are analysis engines, not strategy advisors.

## 5. What consultants actually pay (typical price per seat)

### Takeaway
Three price bands: (a) general assistants and deck tools at $8-40/user/month (Gamma, Pitch, Beautiful.ai, Plus AI, ChatGPT/Claude Team, Perplexity Enterprise Pro); (b) Office-embedded AI at about $30/user/month (Microsoft 365 Copilot) with Claude add-ins included in paid Claude plans; (c) premium research/CI at $10k-50k per seat or contract per year (AlphaSense, Klue, Crayon). Independents typically stay in band (a); one guide estimates an independent stack at about $63-66/month.
