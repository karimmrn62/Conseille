## 1. Decision intelligence (DI) platforms: Aera, Peak/UiPath, Quantexa, Aily Labs, Cloverpop, Tellius, o9, SAP Joule, Oracle, and Gartner's framing

### Takeaway
Gartner now treats "Decision Intelligence Platforms" as an official market (its first Magic Quadrant came out on 26 Jan 2026). The Leaders it names are decisioning or analytics heavyweights (FICO, IBM, SAS, Quantexa), plus Aera according to Aera itself. These products automate or recommend recurring operational decisions (supply chain, pricing, inventory, fraud and KYC). They are sold to large enterprises as six-figure-plus platforms. Almost none of them acts as a cross-functional advisor to a general manager. The closest is Aily Labs, which pushes performance insights to executives. That gap is where Bishop could fit.

### Inferences
- How DI platforms produce recommendations: they ingest ERP, supply chain and transaction data, then run forecasting and optimization models and business rules (more and more with LLM agents on top). The result is a recommendation per decision instance (a reorder, a price, a fraud alert), routed to a planner's inbox or executed automatically. They are narrow and repetitive by design. Bishop's design (reading documents and dashboards, then giving a cross-functional, strategic, narrative recommendation with stakeholder simulation) is close to the opposite end of the spectrum.
- Gartner's DI category is dominated by rules and analytics vendors (FICO, IBM, SAS). This suggests "decision intelligence" as Gartner uses it is about governed operational decisioning, not executive advice. Bishop would sit closer to Gartner's "decision augmentation" idea for managers.
- Aily Labs (about $100M raised, executive performance insights, Fortune 500) is the most direct well-funded comparable for an "AI that tells leaders what is going on and what to do about it".
- Contract sizes: I found no public pricing for Aera, Aily, o9 or Quantexa. Customer profiles (Fortune 500, Mars, Unilever, banks) suggest enterprise contracts. Inference: likely six to seven figures per year, but not sourced.

## 2. EPM / FP&A with AI agents (Pigment, Anaplan, Workday Adaptive, Board, Planful, Cube, Datarails, Abacum, Vena, Oracle): do they recommend operational actions or only forecast and explain variances?

### Takeaway
Through 2025-2026 every EPM vendor shipped "agents". The mainstream feature set is anomaly and variance detection, AI-drafted commentary and reports, natural-language Q&A, and assisted model building. Explicit "recommend strategic or operational actions" is mostly on the roadmap. The clearest example is Pigment's Planner Agent ("simulates scenarios and recommends strategic actions", e.g. regional strategies), whose release status is unclear. The buyer is still the CFO or FP&A team, not the regional director.

### Cited Findings
**Pigment (FR/UK, Paris-founded)**

### Inferences
- Answer to the key question: today these tools mainly forecast, detect, explain and draft. Action recommendation is emerging (Pigment Analyst "suggests actions", Pigment Planner on the roadmap, Datarails "Strategy agent"), and it stays inside the financial model: reallocate budget, run a scenario. None of them reads unstructured strategy documents, models stakeholder reactions, or advises on organizational or people moves. That is still white space.
- Bishop could treat EPM tools as data sources or complements rather than competitors, for example reading Pigment, Anaplan or Adaptive output through MCP or exports. Workday's MCP openness suggests EPM vendors expect outside LLM agents to sit on top of them.
- Pigment is a French unicorn that explicitly courts the "leaders' decision room". It is the most likely large European incumbent to move into "AI that recommends business decisions". Watch its Planner Agent.

### Gaps
- Board, Cube and Mosaic (Mosaic was reportedly acquired by Bob in 2025, not verified here): no current product or funding data retrieved.
- No independent measurement of FP&A agent impact on decision quality was found.
- Pricing is mostly from comparison sites, not vendor list prices.

## 3. Strategy execution / OKR platforms (WorkBoard + Quantive, Cascade, Profit.co, Perdoo, Betterworks): AI that recommends what to do

### Takeaway
WorkBoard, which acquired Quantive in May 2025, ships the most explicit "AI chief of staff for every manager" product. "John", the Chief of Staff agent, and "Sofia", the Leadership Coach, have been GA since May 2025. It recommends where managers should focus, which risks to address and how to coach. The other OKR tools mostly use AI to turn strategy documents into OKRs and plans and to flag risks.

### Cited Findings
- WorkBoard acquired Quantive (maker of "Quantive StrategyAI") on 28 May 2025. Quantive customers are being migrated to WorkBoard, and some Quantive investors took board observer seats. By September 2025, 40 large enterprise customers were migrating. — [WorkBoard PR via Silicon UK](https://www.silicon.co.uk/press-release/workboard-acquires-quantive-to-strengthen-its-strategy-execution-and-okr-software-advantage-and-accelerate-innovation); [Quantive blog](https://prod.quantive.com/resources/blog/workboard-acquires-quantive); [MergerLinks](https://app.mergerlinks.com/transactions/2025-05-28-quantive)

- WorkBoardAI digital workers were announced on 25 Feb 2025 and reached GA on 27 May 2025. The "Chief of Staff" agent handles OKR alignment, risk identification, progress updates and meeting prep. For managers of managers, it helps "calibrate attention and coaching where it will have the most impact". The "Leadership Coach" agent produces weekly summaries of each direct report (achievements, risks, blockers), rewrites feedback in SBI format, suggests who needs coaching or kudos, and drafts 1:1 agendas. ### Inferences
- WorkBoard's "digital Chief of Staff" is the closest existing product to Bishop's "advisor to managers" angle. But its recommendations are bounded by OKR and team data inside WorkBoard: risks, focus, coaching. It does not do business diagnosis, KPI design from scratch, or stakeholder simulation, and it has no strong "personality". Bishop can differentiate on business reasoning depth and a strategic persona.
- Strategy-to-plan generation from documents (Cascade) overlaps with Bishop's "read the situation and propose a plan" feature. Bishop needs to go beyond a format conversion.

### Gaps

## 4. "AI chief of staff", "AI executive advisor", "AI COO" and AI leadership coaches: who gives management or decision advice to managers?

### Takeaway
Two clusters exist. (a) Productivity "chief of staff" agents: meeting and email synthesis, briefings, visibility across the company. Examples are Microsoft 365 Copilot Researcher, WorkBoard, and seed startups like Bond (YC) and Aron. (b) AI leadership coaches that advise managers on people issues, led by Valence's Nadia ($50M Series B, Fortune 500 deployments). No funded product was found that positions itself as an "AI board member" or strategic business advisor to executives. That niche looks empty or occupied only by consultants.

### Cited Findings

Customers include Experian, Delta Air Lines, Prudential, WPP, General Mills and Kraft Heinz. Analog Devices uses it to coach first-time technical managers in 30+ languages. Bessemer says Valence "more than tripled in scale" in a year, and customers report NPS above 90 (self-reported). — [Valence PR](https://www.valence.co/blog/series-b-press-release); [Bessemer](https://www.bvp.com/news/meet-nadia-valences-ai-coach-for-leadership-development-and-workplace-collaboration); [Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/valence-raises-series-b-20250926)

### Inferences
- The term "AI chief of staff" now covers mostly information synthesis and operating-rhythm work. "AI coach" covers mostly people-leadership advice. Business and strategic decision advice with a distinct persona is the less crowded position. Bishop's "visionary chess player" framing sits there.
- Valence shows that enterprises will buy a named AI persona ("Nadia") for managers at scale, with HR or L&D as the buyer. That precedent supports Bishop's persona strategy. Bishop's buyer would more likely be the general management or strategy function, or consulting firms.

### Gaps
- Read AI, Glean agents, Tability, Lattice AI, Rippling, Fireflies, BetterUp AI coaching and CoachHub AIMY were not researched in detail in this pass (no sourced data). In general they fit the meeting-intelligence or coaching clusters above, but this is not verified.
- No funding or pricing found for Bond.

## 5. Products for regional / area managers and multi-site networks that recommend actions (retail, restaurants, etc.)

### Takeaway
This is the most concrete existing case of "AI that tells a field manager what to do". Retail and restaurant operations platforms push prioritized daily or weekly actions to store, district and area managers: YOOBIC Store Manager Copilot and District Manager Copilot, Nory's "AI restaurant manager", and Legion WFM agents. Their recommendations are operational (labour, inventory, compliance, tasks), not strategic.

### Cited Findings
- **YOOBIC** (FR/UK origin): launched "Store Manager Copilot" in Nov 2025 alongside the acquisition of Humanitics. It gives each manager "three priorities for the day with an AI-generated action plan ready to assign", plus natural-language performance insights. A "District Manager Copilot" is listed but not detailed. Area managers run digital store visits and assign action plans. ## 7. Evidence on value and limits: measured impact, failures, Gartner predictions

### Takeaway
Analysts predict very fast growth in AI-augmented decisions (Gartner: 50% of business decisions augmented or automated by AI agents by 2027, and 15% of day-to-day work decisions made autonomously by 2028). The same analysts and academic sources warn of high failure rates: more than 40% of agentic AI projects cancelled by end-2027, and MIT's finding that 95% of GenAI pilots show no rapid P&L impact. Measured impact cases are almost all vendor-reported.

### Cited Findings
- Gartner (data and analytics predictions): by 2027, 50% of business decisions will be augmented or automated by AI agents for decision intelligence. — [Technology Magazine](https://technologymagazine.com/articles/gartner-ai-agents-will-drive-half-of-decisions-by-2027); [Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/agent/by-2027-50-of-business-decisions-will-be-augmented-by-ai-agents-for-decision-intelligence-gartner)
- Gartner (June 2025): at least 15% of day-to-day work decisions will be made autonomously through agentic AI by 2028, up from 0% in 2024. In the same release, more than 40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by end-2027 because of costs, unclear business value or inadequate risk controls. — [Gartner press release 2025-06-25](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027)
- Gartner (March 2026): at least 80% of governments will deploy AI agents to automate routine decision-making by 2028. — [Gartner press release 2026-03-17](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-03-17-gartner-predicts-at-least-80-percent-of-governments-will-deploy-ai-agents-to-automate-routine-decision-making-by-2028)
- Gartner (Nov 2025): by 2028 AI agents will outnumber sellers 10x, yet fewer than 40% of sellers will say agents improved their productivity. This is a signal of the adoption-versus-value gap. — [Gartner press release 2025-11-18](https://gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-11-18-gartner-predicts-by-2028-ai-agents-will-outnumber-sellers-by-10x-yet-fewer-than-40-percent-of-sellers-will-report-ai-agents-improved-productivity)
- MIT NANDA, "The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025" (Aug 2025): 95% of enterprise GenAI pilots stall without measurable rapid P&L impact. The cause is the "learning gap" and poor integration, not model quality. Purchased specialized tools succeed about 67% of the time versus about one-third for internal builds. The method is 150 interviews, a survey of 350 employees and 300 public deployments, and the headline is often over-interpreted. — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/08/18/mit-report-95-percent-generative-ai-pilots-at-companies-failing-cfo/); [Computing](https://www.computing.co.uk/news/2025/ai/mit-report-95pc-corporate-generative-ai-pilots-fail)
- Vendor-reported impact cases: Aera, +20% inventory performance and $1.1M annual spend reduction. Quantexa, 228% three-year ROI (Forrester TEI, commissioned). Legion, 13x ROI. WorkBoard, 75% agent adoption. Valence, NPS above 90. — [Aera](https://www.aeratechnology.com/news/aera-technology-boosts-customer-inventory-performance-by-20percent-with-decision-intelligence/); [Pulse2/Quantexa](https://pulse2.com/quantexa-175-million-series-f-raised-at-2-6-billion-valuation-for-decision-intelligence-solutions); [Legion](https://legion.co/products); [TechIntelPro](https://techintelpro.com/HR/AI/workboard-posts-record-1h-2025-growth-with-ai-agents); [Bessemer](https://www.bvp.com/news/meet-nadia-valences-ai-coach-for-leadership-development-and-workplace-collaboration)
- Qualitative limitation: an analyst described Anaplan CoPlanner as "more like a contextual chatbot than a true decision-making partner". — [QKS Group](https://qksgroup.com/blogs/qks-review-ai-maturity-in-fp-a-who-s-leading-the-intelligence-leveraging-genai-and-who-s-still-stuck-in-spreadsheets-1277)

### Inferences
- Implications for Bishop: (1) The market narrative is favourable (Gartner's "50% of decisions augmented by 2027"). (2) Buyers are becoming sceptical (40% of agentic projects cancelled, MIT's 95%), so Bishop needs a clear value-measurement loop: track which recommendations were adopted and what outcome followed. Even Aera is criticized for weak attribution. (3) The MIT finding that bought, specialized tools beat internal builds supports a packaged advisor, especially sold through consultants who handle integration and the "learning gap".
- Synthesis map, by how far each product goes toward Bishop's job ("read the situation, give KPIs, draw non-obvious conclusions, recommend decisions, simulate stakeholders"):
  - Operational decision automation (narrow, high ROI, enterprise): Aera, Peak/UiPath, o9, FICO, SAS, IBM, Quantexa.
  - Financial planning agents (forecast, variance, scenario; emerging "recommend actions"): Pigment, Anaplan, Workday, Oracle, Planful, Vena, Datarails, Abacum.
  - Strategy execution with manager agents (focus, risks, coaching inside OKR data): WorkBoard (+Quantive), Cascade, Profit.co.
  - Executive insight and performance push (closest well-funded comparable): Aily Labs; Tellius, Cloverpop (smaller).
  - People-leadership advice (persona-based coach): Valence Nadia; WorkBoard Sofia.
  - Frontline and multi-site action prioritization: YOOBIC, Nory, Legion.
  - Generic executive research assistants: Microsoft 365 Copilot Researcher; seed startups (Bond, Aron).
  - Not found as a product: a cross-functional, persona-driven strategic advisor that simulates stakeholder reactions for regional directors, BU heads or leadership teams. This is Bishop's potential white space (inference from absence of evidence; coverage is not exhaustive).
